# Channel 2 — FOUNDER · "Day one of building the app"

**You on camera. 25 seconds. Filmed on your phone. Cost: $0.**

Nobody can generate this for you — an AI pretending to be the founder is exactly the
fabrication the whole playbook forbids. It's also the easiest video here: one take, no edit.

## Setup (2 minutes)
- Phone propped against something at desk height, front camera, **vertical**
- Sit where you actually work. Messy desk is good. Don't tidy it.
- Daylight from a window **in front of you**, not behind
- No script reading. Learn the beats, then talk.

## The beats (don't memorise word-for-word)

> **0–4s — the confession, not the pitch**
> "I built an app because I couldn't stop opening Instagram. Nine hours some days."
>
> **4–12s — what it does, plainly**
> "It blocks the apps you pick. But the part that matters — you can't unlock them yourself.
> You send a request to two or three friends, and they decide."
>
> **12–20s — the honest bit (this is what makes it work)**
> "It's not on the App Store yet. I'm waiting on an Apple entitlement, which takes weeks.
> So right now it's just me and a waitlist."
>
> **20–25s — ask**
> "If you'd use something like this — link's in my bio. I'll tell you when it's out."

## Why this converts
The admission that it isn't shipped is the strongest thing in the video. Build-in-public
audiences reward the person who says the unflattering true thing. It also makes the waitlist
CTA honest instead of a bait — you're not asking for a download that doesn't exist.

## Hard rules
- ❌ Don't claim users, results, or screen-time drops. There are none yet.
- ❌ Don't demo the friend-denial flow. It isn't wired.
- ✅ Do show the real locked screen if you show anything.
- ✅ One take. Stumbling is fine. Re-recording five times makes it worse.

## Caption
`day 1 of building an app that locks your phone until a friend says yes 🔒`

# Channel 3 — BRAND · Silent product loop

**No face, no voice, no AI. Real app screenshots + motion + text. Cost: $0.**

The brand account is low-volume and exists for credibility — someone hears about LockIn,
searches it, and finds something real. This is that asset.

## Source material (already captured, non-zero numbers)
```
content/assets/home.png     → 5 LOCKED IN · Petr + Jakub orbiting · 5 BLOCKED / 12 DAY STREAK
content/assets/locked.png   → "5 apps · 1h 2m" + "The only way back in is to ask Petr or Jakub."
```

## Structure (12s, silent, trending audio added in-app)

| Time | Frame | Text overlay |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 0–3s | `locked.png`, slow push-in on the lock | **you can't unlock it** |
| 3–6s | hold on *"ask Petr or Jakub. They hold the key."* | **they can say no** |
| 6–9s | `home.png`, push-in on the focus ring | **2–4 people. that's the app.** |
| 9–12s | end card, warm dark, flame | **LockIn · early access · locked-in.dev** |

## Why silent
It gets watched on mute, it costs nothing, it can't be AI-flagged, and the copy on your own
locked screen is already the best line in the product. Let it carry the video.

## Assembly
CapCut: import both PNGs → Ken Burns push-in on each → text overlays in the Ember yellow
(`#FFD60A`) → trending audio at low volume → export 1080×1920.

## Posting order

1. **Founder** first — it's honest, free, and warms the account before anything promotional
2. **Brand** second — the credibility asset sits there and waits
3. **Persona (reel 01)** — the AI one, labeled, treated as paid/test creative

Track waitlist signups per account with the UTM tags in the account plan. Never views.
