# Content Strategy Playbook (Verified 2026)

Output of a deep-research harness: 28 sources fetched, 25 claims adversarially verified (2-of-3 votes to kill), 10 claims killed. Confidence levels noted per claim. **Do not trust the killed claims** (listed at the bottom) — they circulate widely and are wrong.

---

## ⚠️ The binding constraint: TikTok's 2026 AI-content policy

**AI-generated realistic visuals/audio MUST carry a visible AI label. Unlabeled synthetic media is auto-flagged, reach-reduced, or removed** (C2PA Content Credentials, live since Jan 2025, scanning 1.3B+ videos). **BUT AI-assisted scripts, hooks, briefs, captions are exempt.** [Confidence: HIGH — TikTok primary policy + storrito + auditsocials]

- AI to write a hook = no label. AI to generate a synthetic person = label required.
- **Implication:** the safe, higher-reach stack is **human / screen-recording video, with AI confined to the writing/strategy layer.** An AI talking-head organically either gets labeled (killing the "real person" trust) or suppressed for hiding it.
- Reserve AI-generated video (fal/openshorts/Higgs Field) for **paid ads** (label matters less) or the **B-roll layer**.

## The 5 highest-leverage moves (ranked by ROI, solo founder ~$0–100/mo)

### 1. 🥇 Persona-led education account — the proven pattern
Opal's own social manager runs **"Olivia Unplugged"** (~402K followers) as an apparently-independent creator leading with *education* ("how to cure your phone addiction"), branding almost invisible, product mentioned only after trust. **~8M views in 30 days** (one video 2.6M); Opal confirmed on record it drove real installs, trials, and revenue. [Confidence: HIGH — AP wire + milkkarten case study]
→ Free, directly replicable. Become "the [topic] person," not "the [product] founder."

### 2. 🥈 Founder build-in-public + waitlist (pre-launch)
Before the product ships, post the *build*, not staged feature demos. Founder story + confessions + "I'm making X." Every video → waitlist. Honest content that works while the app isn't live.

### 3. 🥉 Pain-point hooks, human/screen-recording format
Pain-point hooks beat benefit hooks (retention edge, loss-aversion) — but **test both**. [Confidence: MEDIUM — marketing blogs, not A/B studies]
Templates to test first:
- **Pain-Point:** "Still [problem] even though you [tried the obvious fix]?"
- **Challenge:** "I did [hard thing] for 30 days."
- **Data-Backed:** "I tested 6 [category] apps — here's the only one that worked."
- **Before/After:** the real screenshot / number transformation.

### 4. Seeded virality + micro-creators (when you have ~$100)
Reference playbook: **BeReal's campus mechanics** — paid ambassadors (~$30/referral), campus parties gating free entry on *download + add friends*. [Confidence: HIGH; exact $ single-sourced]
**Counter-signal:** Nikita Bier's first-party data — invites/user drop ~20% per year of age from 13→18. **A 17–30 audience is the low-virality end → budget for seeding, don't rely on pure organic sharing.** [Confidence: HIGH]

### 5. Reddit + niche communities (parallel, $0)
r/nosurf, r/digitalminimalism, r/getdisciplined (adapt to your niche). Self-selected buyers, high intent, low volume. Honest founder posts.

## Budget benchmarks
- Micro-influencers (10K–100K): **$100–500/IG post, $200–800/TikTok video** (up to ~$500–2,000/campaign). [Confidence: HIGH — Influencer Marketing Hub 2026]
- At ~$100/mo one post = whole budget → **favor pay-per-install / pay-per-view deals + nano-creators**, not one-off flat fees. Spark Ads add ~20–50%.

## Measurement
- Optimize **waitlist signups → installs, NEVER views.**
- Model paid tests against **~1.92% conversion** for conversion-optimized TikTok ads. [Confidence: MEDIUM — single vendor]
- ⚠️ CTR/CPM benchmarks from the same vendor were **REFUTED** — gather your own.

## 30 / 60 / 90-day plan

**Days 1–30 — Seed identity + waitlist (product not live)**
- 1 persona/founder account each on TikTok + IG. 1 post/day, manual publish from phone.
- Human / screen-recording / screenshot format. AI writes hooks only.
- Test all 4 hook templates × core angles. Bio → waitlist. Goal: 2–3 formats break 10k views.

**Days 31–60 — Double down + community**
- 10× the winning formats (AI generates variants). Kill the rest.
- Start Reddit/community posting. Open a Discord for the waitlist.
- If in TestFlight: honest "here's it working" demos — **only once the core feature actually works.**

**Days 61–90 — Launch + paid amplification**
- Product live → flip waitlist to installs. Hero content = the *real* signature moment (only because it now works).
- First $100 → 1–2 nano-creators on performance deals + boost top organic post as a Spark Ad.
- Weekly loop: top posts by *signups/installs* → 20 variants → scale.

---

## ❌ Do NOT
- Lead with AI talking-head UGC organically (label + suppression).
- Stage the product's signature moment before the feature works (integrity + it flops).
- Chase views. 1M views + 0 installs = worthless.
