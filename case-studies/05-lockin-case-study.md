# Case Study: Applying the Machine to LockIn

Worked example of the playbook on a real product. **LockIn** = a pre-launch iOS focus app that blocks distracting apps via Apple Screen Time; its differentiator is a "pact" of 2–4 friends who must approve any unlock — friends literally hold the key.

> This is the *application* layer. The reusable machine lives in `research/` and `strategy/`. LockIn's actual app development stays in its own repo — this is only the content/marketing angle.

## Core insight
Focus apps have an unfair UGC advantage: **the pain is the content.** The buyer is already on TikTok/Reels feeling guilty about being on TikTok/Reels. You sell the exit door to the exact room they're standing in. The whole move: **catch someone mid-doomscroll, name their shame precisely, show the escape.** Shame → relief → proof. No feature tours.

## Personas → content lanes

| Persona | Pain | Hook that stops them |
|---|---|---|
| Student (16–22, examTok/studyTok) | "Open phone to check the time, lose 2 hours. Exams in 3 weeks." | screen-time shame + grades |
| Self-improvement / "lock in" / 5am | "Say I'll lock in, then reels for 4 hours." | discipline / identity |
| Doomscroll-guilt normie (17–30) | "Hate how much I'm on my phone, can't stop." | relatable self-roast |

"Lock in" is already a viral Gen Z phrase — the product name IS a trending caption.

## The 6 angles to test
1. **Screen-time reveal** — "I checked my screen time and almost threw up" → the number → LockIn.
2. **Before/After** — "8h TikTok/day → 45 min. one change."
3. **POV roast** — "POV: open phone to check one text, it's 2 hours later."
4. **Mechanism demo** — "my friends literally hold the key to my Instagram now."
5. **Denial moment ⭐** — "I begged my friend to unlock TikTok. she said no." (the only real differentiator — but do NOT run it until the unlock feature actually works; never fake the core moment)
6. **Anti-competitor confession** — "I tried Opal, one sec, Forest. deleted all three. this stuck."

## Hook bank
- "I checked my screen time and I need to lie down."
- "this is your sign to lock the f*** in."
- "no one's coming to save you from your For You page. so I did this."
- "how I went from 9 hours of TikTok to actually passing my exams."
- "if you pick up your phone every 3 minutes, this is an intervention."
- "I deleted Instagram 6 times. it didn't work. this did."
- "POV: you finally stopped letting your phone run your life."

## Fully-spec'd first reel (screen-time reveal)

**Hook (0–3s):** "I checked my screen time this morning and genuinely almost cried."

**Script (~30s):**
> "I checked my screen time this morning and genuinely almost cried. [beat] Nine hours. On my phone. Seven of it on TikTok and Instagram. I have exams in three weeks. [beat] And the worst part — I'd open my phone to check ONE thing and disappear for two hours. Deleting the apps never worked, I'd redownload them in a day. So I got this thing called LockIn. It straight up locks the apps — I set the hours, and when I try to open Instagram it just won't let me. First day my screen time dropped to two hours and I actually studied. It's free. Lock in before I did. Link's in my bio."

**Voiceover:** casual, slightly defeated → relieved. "Talking to a friend who caught you slipping." Real sigh on "nine hours."

**Captions (big, key words only):** "9 HOURS 😭" → "exams in 3 weeks" → "deleting apps NEVER worked" → "LockIn" → "screen time: 2h ✅" → "free. link in bio."

**Cheapest production (recommended):** film it yourself on your phone OR a real screen-time screenshot carousel + trending audio. **$0, no AI-labeling penalty.** Only reach for fal/openshorts AI-actor for paid ads.

## LockIn-specific application of the playbook
- **Move #1 (persona-led education):** run a "phone addiction / how to actually lock in" account, not a "LockIn founder" account.
- **Move #2 (build-in-public):** pre-launch, post the build + waitlist at locked-in.dev.
- **Constraint:** pact-invite virality is weak at 17–30 (Bier decay) → plan to seed, not rely on organic.
- **Sequence:** entitlement + backend live → fix the unlock flow → *then* the denial-moment reels (angle #5) become unlockable hero content.

## Production loop
1. Batch 6 angles × 3 hooks = ~18 first reels (cheapest format first, ~$0–18).
2. Post across a few fresh accounts, manual publish, bio → waitlist.
3. Friday loop: top by *signups*, generate 20 variants of the winner, kill the rest.
4. Scale only what converts to installs, not views.
